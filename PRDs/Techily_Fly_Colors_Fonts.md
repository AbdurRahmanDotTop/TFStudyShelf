The visual system is intentionally narrow. The goal is **consistency, recognition, accessibility, scalability, and prevention of visual drift**.

### Core identity

> **Serious technology. Intelligent systems. Human energy.**

### Mandatory primary palette

| Brand token | Hex |
|---|---|
| TF Graphite | `#212121` |
| Signal Coral | `#FF7759` |
| Cloud Surface | `#FAFAFA` |

### Mandatory typography

| Role | Typeface |
|---|---|
| Brand, headings, UI, body | **Manrope** |
| Technical, code, machine/data layer | **Geist Mono** |

No other font family may be introduced without an explicit design-system revision.
